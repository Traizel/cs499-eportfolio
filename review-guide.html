Review process and final decisions

This guide supports review of individual capstone work. It distinguishes checks performed during final assembly from future feedback and environment-specific testing.

How to review a change

Start with the intended behavior and original source. Compare the enhanced implementation, read its narrative, then run the relevant tests. Record each concern with a file or function, the observed behavior, the expected behavior, and a suggested check. Discuss behavior and evidence rather than personal preferences. A written response should state the accepted change or explain the tradeoff behind retaining the implementation.

Audiences and decisions

A rescue coordinator needs to understand what a candidate filter includes and why a match does not guarantee suitability. A developer needs the repository contract, result types, and migration steps. A database administrator needs index definitions and permission requirements. Written explanations, source views, and the video allow reviewers to choose an appropriate level of detail.
