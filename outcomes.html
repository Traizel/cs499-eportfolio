Where to find the evidence

The five outcomes are assessed across the portfolio. This map identifies the specific materials and the limits of their evidence.

OutcomePortfolio focusEvidence
1 · CollaborationUse review strategies that make decisions accessible to different audiences.Review guide; self-assessment discussion of review requests, stakeholder needs, written notes, and walkthroughs. Individual coursework; external collaboration is not claimed.
2 · CommunicationExplain the work through oral, written, and visual material suited to the audience.Code review video; software narrative; algorithms narrative; database narrative; navigable source views and run instructions.
3 · Computing solutionsSelect algorithms and structures and evaluate their tradeoffs.Heap scheduler, deque, rolling sum, and hysteresis; index comparison; migration, smoothing delay, scheduling overruns, and pagination limitations.
4 · Practical tools and implementationBuild working software with appropriate computing techniques.Layered Python package and PyMongo adapter; 19 thermostat tests; 45 database-package tests; simulator and retained live verification.
5 · Security mindsetAnticipate misuse and failures and limit their effects.Approved fields, targeted mutations, configuration, and controlled exceptions; bounded search policy; sensor-fault handling and bounded queue. Server roles and deployment permissions remain separate controls.
